## Searcher design decision

Split search into:

- `FixedSpecSearcher`: brute-force/filtered fixedSeed search after GenerationSpec policies are fixed.
- `EncounterStateSearcher`: outer `s0/s1` search preserving natural encounter slot, level, policies and mark.

This prevents the incorrect assumption that one fixed seed determines the entire overworld encounter.
